# Title Entity

## Purpose

Represents every watchable horror title in HorrorJungle.

Examples

- Hereditary
- The Conjuring
- From
- Midnight Mass
- Junji Ito Collection

---

## Supported Types

- Movie
- TV Series
- Anime
- Documentary
- Short Film
- TV Special

---

## Responsibilities

Stores only information intrinsic to the title.

- Name
- Release Date
- Runtime
- Synopsis
- Poster
- Banner
- Trailer
- Country
- Language
- Status
- SEO

Does NOT store

- Reviews
- Ending Explained
- Genres
- Characters
- Platforms
- Director
